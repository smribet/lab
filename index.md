# About us

We are an electron microscopy research group based at the National Center for Electron Microscopy within the Molecular Foundry at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. Our work lies at the intersection of computational methods, experimental science, and hardware development. As part of the Department of Energy’s ***Nanoscale Science Research Centers*** network, we collaborate with researchers from around the world and provide access to advanced electron microscopy capabilities—learn more on our [Users @ the MF](./users.md) page.
