We are an electron microscopy research group based at the National Center for Electron Microscopy within the Molecular Foundry at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. Our work lies at the intersection of computational methods, experimental science, and hardware development. Check out our [Research](./research.md) page to learn more about the methods that we are developing. As part of the Department of Energy’s Nanoscale Science Research Centers network, we collaborate with researchers from around the world and provide access to advanced electron microscopy capabilities. Click to learn more about the [Molecular Foundry User Program](./users.md).
